# Toy Sales Analysis

## Business Question

How do sales and quantities vary across different toy products, and which products contribute the most to overall sales?

The analysis examines product-level sales and quantity data to identify differences in sales performance and understand the contribution of each item to total sales.

## 1. Dataset

The project uses a toy sales dataset stored in:

`toy_sales_data.csv`

The dataset contains information including:

- Date
- Item
- Quantity
- Total Sales

The analysis focuses primarily on **Item**, **Quantity**, and **Total Sales** to evaluate product-level sales performance.

## 2. Tools

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## 3. Data Cleaning

The following data preparation steps were performed:

- Loaded the CSV dataset using Pandas.
- Inspected the data types of the `Date` and `Total Sales` columns.
- Removed the `$` symbol from the `Total Sales` column.
- Converted `Total Sales` from a string/object data type to an integer.
- Grouped sales data by `Item` to calculate total sales for each product.
- Grouped quantity data by `Item` to calculate total quantity sold for each product.

## 4. Analysis

The analysis focused on comparing sales and quantity across toy products.

### Product Sales

Total sales were aggregated by product using Pandas `groupby()` to determine the revenue generated by each item.

### Sales Contribution

The percentage contribution of each product to total sales was calculated and presented using a pie chart.

### Sales Comparison

Product-level sales were analyzed using both bar and line plots to compare the revenue generated by each item.

### Quantity Analysis

The total quantity sold for each product was calculated and analyzed to compare sales volume across items.

## 5. Visualizations

The project uses several visualization techniques:

- **Bar chart** — compares total sales across products.
- **Pie chart** — shows each product's percentage contribution to total sales.
- **Line plot** — compares total sales across products.
- **Combined bar and line plots** — provides two views of product-level sales performance.
- **Seaborn scatter plot** — visualizes the quantity sold for each product.

## 6. Key Findings

- Sales performance varies across the different toy products.
- The contribution of individual products to total sales can be compared using their percentage share of overall sales.
- The bar and line visualizations provide a clear comparison of revenue generated by each product.
- Quantity sold varies across products, highlighting differences in sales volume.
- Comparing **revenue and quantity sold together** provides a more complete view of product performance than looking at either metric independently.
