# Retail Sales & Returns Performance Dashboard

## Business Question

How are sales, transactions, product demand, customer activity, and product returns performing over time, and which products or categories contribute most to business performance?

This dashboard helps management monitor revenue generation, transaction activity, product demand, customer engagement, and return behavior to support operational and commercial decision-making.

## 1. Dataset

The dashboard uses a retail sales dataset consisting of:

- Customer information
- Product information
- Product categories
- Sales transactions
- Quantity ordered
- Product returns
- Calendar dates

Key business dimensions include:

- Customers
- Products
- Product Categories
- Date (Year, Quarter, Month, Weekday)

## 2. Tools

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling
- Interactive Dashboard Design

## 3. Data Cleaning

The data preparation process included:

- Creating relationships between sales, products, customers, and calendar tables.
- Building a Calendar table to support time-based analysis.
- Organizing customer and product information into lookup tables.
- Creating DAX measures for sales, transactions, quantities, returns, and rankings.
- Structuring the model for interactive filtering and drill-down analysis.

## 4. Analysis

### Sales Performance Analysis

The dashboard tracks:

- Net Sales
- Gross Revenue
- Gross Profit Margin

These measures provide insight into overall business performance and profitability.

### Transaction Analysis

Transaction activity is monitored using:

- Total Transactions
- Number of Customers
- Number of Products

This helps evaluate customer engagement and sales activity.

### Quantity Ordered Analysis

The report analyzes:

- Total Quantity Ordered
- Product demand trends
- Category-level sales performance

This helps identify high-performing products and categories.

### Customer Analysis

Customer performance is evaluated through:

- Customer counts
- Customer ratings
- Purchasing activity

This supports customer segmentation and performance monitoring.

### Returns Analysis

The dashboard tracks:

- Quantity Returned
- Total Refund Value
- Refund Rate (%)

This helps identify products or categories generating excessive returns.

### Product Ranking

Products are ranked based on performance metrics, enabling users to identify:

- Best-performing products
- Underperforming products
- Products generating high return rates

## 6. Key Findings

- Sales performance can be monitored through Net Sales and Gross Revenue metrics.
- Product demand patterns can be identified using quantity ordered trends.
- Customer activity can be evaluated through transaction and customer metrics.
- Product categories contribute differently to overall business performance.
- Return rates vary across products and categories.
- High-return products can be identified and investigated.
- Time-based analysis highlights seasonal and periodic performance patterns.
- Product ranking analysis helps prioritize high-performing products and address underperforming items.

## Business Value

This dashboard enables management to:

- Monitor sales performance in real time.
- Identify high-performing products and categories.
- Track customer activity and purchasing behavior.
- Reduce losses associated with excessive product returns.
- Improve inventory planning using quantity demand trends.
- Support data-driven sales and operational decisions.
