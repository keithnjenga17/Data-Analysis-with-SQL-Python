# Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis

## Business Question

Which products, regions, sales representatives, and periods are driving sales performance, and where should the business focus its future sales and investment efforts?

The analysis evaluates sales revenue, product performance, regional performance, sales representative contribution, and sales trends throughout 2024 to identify the main drivers of revenue and potential areas for improvement.

## 1. Dataset

The dataset contains **1,000 sales transactions** covering the period from **January 1, 2024 to December 30, 2024**.

Each transaction contains information on:

- Date
- Product
- Category
- Region
- Sales Representative
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Sales Amount
- Day

### Dataset Coverage

- **10 products**
- **3 product categories**
- **4 regions**
- **5 sales representatives**
- **9,970 units sold**
- **1,000 sales transactions**

The total recorded sales amount was approximately:

**₦32.42 million**

The average sales amount per transaction was approximately:

**₦32,423**

## 4. Analysis

### Product Performance

Product sales were analyzed to identify the products contributing most to total revenue.

The highest-performing products were:

| Product | Sales Contribution |
|---|---:|
| Rice (5kg) | 48.14% |
| Chicken | 15.06% |
| Eggs (crate) | 9.25% |
| Cooking Oil (1L) | 9.01% |

**Rice (5kg)** was the dominant product, generating approximately **₦15.61 million**, equivalent to **48.14% of total sales**.

This makes Rice (5kg) the most significant product in the current sales portfolio.

### Category Performance

Sales were analyzed across three categories:

| Category | Sales | Contribution |
|---|---:|---:|
| Food | ₦11.76M | 36.28% |
| Groceries | ₦11.28M | 34.78% |
| Beverage | ₦9.38M | 28.94% |

Food generated the highest sales contribution, followed closely by Groceries.

### Regional Performance

Sales were compared across four regions:

| Region | Sales | Contribution |
|---|---:|---:|
| Kano | ₦9.64M | 29.73% |
| Port Harcourt | ₦7.78M | 23.99% |
| Abuja | ₦7.77M | 23.97% |
| Lagos | ₦7.23M | 22.31% |

**Kano was the strongest-performing region**, contributing approximately 29.73% of total sales.

**Lagos was the lowest-performing region**, contributing approximately 22.31%.

The difference between the highest and lowest performing regions is approximately **₦2.41 million**, suggesting an opportunity to investigate what is driving Kano's stronger performance and whether similar practices could improve Lagos performance.

### Sales Representative Performance

Sales were analyzed across five sales representatives:

| Sales Representative | Sales | Contribution |
|---|---:|---:|
| David | ₦8.03M | 24.77% |
| Amina | ₦6.70M | 20.68% |
| John | ₦6.38M | 19.68% |
| Chioma | ₦5.65M | 17.44% |
| Ibrahim | ₦5.65M | 17.43% |

**David was the highest-performing sales representative**, contributing approximately ₦8.03 million, or 24.77% of total sales.

Ibrahim recorded the lowest sales contribution at approximately 17.43%.

### Monthly Sales Trends

Sales were analyzed across the 12 months of 2024.

The strongest month was:

**August — ₦4.30 million**

The weakest month was:

**January — ₦1.86 million**

This indicates considerable variation in monthly sales performance.

August sales were approximately **2.32 times January sales**, making seasonal or monthly sales patterns an important area for further investigation.

## 6. Key Findings

- Total sales were approximately **₦32.42 million** across 1,000 transactions.
- **Rice (5kg) was the dominant product**, contributing approximately **48.14% of total sales**.
- **Food was the largest category**, contributing approximately **36.28% of total sales**.
- **Kano was the strongest-performing region**, contributing approximately **29.73% of total sales**.
- **Lagos was the lowest-performing region**, contributing approximately **22.31%**.
- **David was the highest-performing sales representative**, contributing approximately **24.77% of total sales**.
- **August generated the highest monthly sales**, at approximately **₦4.30 million**.
- **January generated the lowest monthly sales**, at approximately **₦1.86 million**.
- Sales performance is highly concentrated around a few products, particularly Rice (5kg), creating both a strong revenue driver and a potential concentration risk.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Protect and optimize Rice (5kg) sales** because it contributes almost half of total revenue. Inventory availability and pricing should be monitored closely.

2. **Investigate Kano's performance** to understand the sales practices, customer demand, pricing, or distribution factors contributing to its stronger results.

3. **Develop a targeted strategy for Lagos**, the lowest-performing region, focusing on customer demand, sales coverage, product mix, and sales representative performance.

4. **Study David's sales approach** and identify practices that could be shared with lower-performing sales representatives.

5. **Investigate seasonal sales patterns**, particularly the significant difference between January and August, to improve inventory and sales planning.

6. **Reduce revenue concentration risk** by developing stronger sales performance across products other than Rice (5kg).
